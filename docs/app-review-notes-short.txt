ACCESS: Demo credentials are in App Review Information > Sign-In Information.
On the sign-in screen, tick the agreement to the Terms of Service and Privacy
Policy — every sign-in option is disabled until it is ticked — then tap "Sign in
with email" beneath the two buttons. Sign in with Apple and Google are also
offered; both create a fresh, empty account. The demo account is pre-populated
with accepted friends, a feed of their sessions, its own sessions with photos,
goals and habits, and it starts at onboarding so the first-run flow can be seen.

MAIN FLOW: Sign in > onboarding creates a first goal and habit (notification
permission is requested here and may be declined) > Clock tab: pick a category,
"Clock in", pause/resume, clock out, attach a note and photo > Progress tab:
habits, goals, weekly recap > Friends: search a username and send a request >
Feed: reactions and comments > You > Settings: preferences and account deletion.
Reporting and blocking are on every profile, session and feed card. Feeds are
row-level-security gated, so an account with no accepted friends correctly sees
an empty feed — intended, not a failure.

DEVICES: iPhone-only (TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1), minimum iOS 15.0. Tested on
<DEVICE MODEL> running iOS <VERSION>; all recorded flows verified on hardware.

WHAT IT IS: A productivity tracker with a social layer. Users clock in to a
category of work; finished sessions appear in a feed their accepted friends can
react to and comment on, alongside weekly goals, daily habits and a weekly
recap. For students and self-directed knowledge workers who want accountability
from people they know: solo time tracking is easy to abandon because nothing
happens when you stop. Free — no in-app purchases, subscriptions or advertising.

SERVICES: Supabase (database, auth, photo storage), Vercel (hosting), Sign in
with Apple and Google Sign-In (authentication), Google Calendar API read-only
(optional), Anthropic Claude API (categorizes calendar event titles; not used
for advertising or model training), APNs (notifications), PostHog (analytics
keyed to user ID; no advertising identifiers, no tracking, no data brokers). No
payment processor, ad network or data provider.

REGIONS: No regional differences. Identical in every storefront, English-only,
no geofenced content, no region-specific pricing. Time zone is user-selected in
Settings.

REGULATED / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL: None applicable. Not a health, medical,
financial or gambling product; work-time entries are user content, not health
data. No licensed third-party material. The only third-party user data touched
is the user's own Google Calendar, read-only under OAuth consent and governed by
the Google API Services User Data Policy, committed to at
https://progra.world/privacy.
