3. WHAT IT DOES / WHO IT IS FOR
Progra is a productivity tracker with a social layer. A user clocks in to a category of work, the app times the session, and the finished session — with an optional note and photo — appears in a feed their accepted friends can see, react to and comment on. Around that loop it keeps weekly goals, daily habits, session history, and a weekly recap.
Audience: students and self-directed knowledge workers who track their own time and want accountability from people they know.
Problem and value: solo time tracking is easy to abandon because nothing happens when you stop. Progra makes tracked time visible to a small, mutual-consent friend group, so the user ends the week with an honest record and a peer group that saw the work.
Free. No in-app purchases, no subscription, no advertising.

4. SETUP / ACCESS
There is no demo account and none is possible: Progra has no username/password path. Sign-in is Sign in with Apple or Google only, so a reviewer signs in with their own Apple ID and gets a fresh account. Hide My Email is supported; no invite code or sample files are needed.
1) Launch, tap "Sign in with Apple".
2) Onboarding runs once and creates a first goal and habit. Notification permission is requested here and may be declined without blocking anything.
3) Clock (center tab): pick a category, tap "Clock in". Pause/resume supported; clocking out opens a finish screen for a note and photo.
4) Progress (first tab): habits, goals, weekly recap. Feed: reactions and comments on friends' sessions.
5) Friends: search a username and send a request. Both sides must accept before either can see the other's sessions. To see the social surfaces populated, add <HELPER HANDLE>, which accepts promptly and has visible sessions.
6) You > Settings: profile, time zone, notification preferences, bug report, and account deletion at the bottom.
Social surfaces are gated in the database by row-level security on auth.uid(), so a new account correctly sees an empty feed until a friend request is accepted. That is the intended state, not a failure.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage): database, authentication, private photo storage
- Vercel: hosting for the web layer the native shell loads
- Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In: authentication
- Google Calendar API (calendar.events.readonly): optional; reads event titles and times so calendar time can sit beside tracked time
- Anthropic (Claude API): categorizes calendar event titles into the user's own categories; not used for advertising or model training
- Apple Push Notification service: social notifications
- PostHog: product analytics keyed to the account's user ID; no advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, no data brokers
No payment processor, ad network or data provider, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Progra behaves identically in every region and storefront. The interface is English-only. There is no geofenced content, no region-specific pricing (the app is free), and no feature enabled or disabled by country. Times display in the time zone chosen in Settings, the only locale-dependent behavior in the app.
